Choose Your Membership

Join Readers’ Haven and borrow books all year round. Pick the plan that fits you. All fees are yearly and are paid in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

Student Plan

PKR 1,000 / year

  • Borrowing limit3 books at a time
  • Loan period14 days
  • Late returnPKR 10 per book per day
  • Who can joinStudents with a valid student ID

Adult Plan

PKR 2,500 / year

  • Borrowing limit5 books at a time
  • Loan period21 days
  • Late returnPKR 20 per book per day
  • Who can joinAnyone aged 18 or above with a CNIC
Best for households

Family Plan

PKR 4,500 / year

  • Borrowing limit10 books at a time, shared by up to 4 family members
  • Loan period21 days
  • Late returnPKR 20 per book per day
  • Who can joinParents and children living at the same address

Late-Return Rules

  • Fines start the day after the due date.
  • You can renew a book once for the same loan period if no one else has reserved it.
  • You cannot borrow new books until all fines are paid.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.
