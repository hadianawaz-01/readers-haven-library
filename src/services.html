Digital Services

Everything you can do online or on our digital floor.

Online Catalogue

Search our full collection from home.

  1. Open the catalogue page.
  2. Search by title, author or ISBN.
  3. Check availability and reserve the book.

E-Books

Borrow digital books with your membership number.

  1. Sign in with your membership number.
  2. Browse and borrow a title.
  3. Download it; it returns automatically after 14 days.

Study-Room Booking

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

  1. Choose a date and a time slot.
  2. Confirm with your membership number.
  3. Maximum booking: 2 hours per day.

Printing and Scanning

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

  • Black and white print: PKR 10 per page.
  • Colour print: PKR 30 per page.
  • Scan to email: free.

Free Wi-Fi

Timings: all opening hours.

  1. Select the network ReadersHaven-Guest.
  2. Open a browser and accept the terms.
  3. Fair-use limit: 2 GB per visitor per day.
